Baikunthpur Block (Gopalganj District)

Main Markets

Dighwa Dubauli

Dighwa Dubauli is one of the largest commercial hubs of Baikunthpur. It serves as a vibrant marketplace for agricultural products, textiles, and daily essentials. The market is always bustling and acts as a lifeline for trade in the region.

Mohammadpur

Mohammadpur is another prominent marketplace of Baikunthpur, known for its wholesale trade in grains, vegetables, and household items. It connects nearby villages to the local economy and draws heavy footfall during weekly markets.

Railway Connectivity

The nearest and most important station is Dighwa Dubauli Railway Station, which connects Baikunthpur to Gopalganj, Chapra, and Thawe. It is a vital route for both passengers and goods.

🎓 Educational Hub

Braj Kishor Narayan Singh Government Polytechnic, Gopalganj

Braj Kishor Narayan Singh Government Polytechnic (BKNSGP), Gopalganj is a government-run technical institute established in 2016 under the Department of Science & Technology, Bihar. It is AICTE-approved and affiliated to SBTE, Bihar.Located at Mantengrahi, near Baikunthpur (Gopalganj), the college offers 3-year diploma programs in Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, and Computer Science Engineering with an intake of around 60–120 seats per branch.Admissions are through the DCECE entrance exam conducted by the BCECE Board. The campus has hostels, labs, library, sports facilities, and workshops, along with skill development programs under the Bihar Skill Development Mission.

Tourist Attractions

Dighwa Dubauli Mounds

These ancient mounds are an archaeological site believed to hold remnants of early human settlement. They reflect the historical significance of Baikunthpur and are often visited by researchers and history enthusiasts. but not confirmed yet. The mounds are located near the Dighwa Dubauli village and are a part of the rich cultural heritage of the region.

Vaman Gandey Ponds (Dighwa Dubauli)

The Vaman Gandey ponds are a serene attraction known for their cultural value and scenic beauty. Surrounded by greenery, these ponds are a place of local gatherings and festivals.

>

Baikunthpur Bird Sanctuary

A natural paradise for bird lovers, this sanctuary is home to numerous local and migratory bird species. During winter, thousands of birds can be spotted, making it a popular destination for eco-tourism.

Sigashani (Chiutahan) Dham

Sigashani Dham is a famous spiritual and cultural site in Baikunthpur. Devotees from nearby districts visit this temple to offer prayers and participate in religious events, making it a center of local faith.

Ram Ghat (Dumariya Ghat)

Located along the Gandak riverfront, Ram Ghat—also known as Dumariya Ghat—is a newly developed site. It has separate bathing steps for men (Ram Ghat) and women (Sita Ghat). The ghat is especially crowded during Kartik Purnima and other festivals, attracting people from Gopalganj, Siwan, Chapra, and Muzaffarpur. Under the Namami Gange project, modern facilities including electric crematoria are being added, enhancing its religious and cultural importance.
