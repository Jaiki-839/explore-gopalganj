Barauli Block (Gopalganj District)

Main Markets

Barauli

Barauli is known for its Barauli Bazaar, which is the main commercial hub. The town has a dedicated vegetable market (Sabzi Mandi) and other busy , shopping areas like Station Road Bazaar and Thana Road Bazaar. Weekly markets (haats) are also organized, where villagers from nearby areas come to trade. The economy is mainly driven by retail business, small trade, and agriculture support markets.

🎓 Educational Hub

National Barauli College

The main institute here is Natioanal Barauli College, which is a degree college affiliated with Jai Prakash University, Chapra. It offers undergraduate programs (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com) and intermediate (+2) level education. Many nearby village students come here for graduation in arts, commerce, and science. For technical or medical studies, students usually go to Baikunthpur (Polytechnic), Gopalganj town, Siwan, or Patna.
